# Pre-cropped screenshots

The screenshots in `../full/` are 16:9 (1920×1080). Articles, social cards and video
thumbnails usually crop to something wider — and a centre crop takes it off the bottom
of the frame.

**The bottom edge is where this game shows what it is.** Both players commit a move
face-down, and the two cards flip at the same moment; those cards sit along the bottom
of the screen. Centre-crop a 16:9 frame to 2.35:1 and the question marks are cut in
half — what's left is two characters standing in a room.

So here are versions we cropped ourselves, keeping the bottom edge intact:

| Suffix | Aspect | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| `_1.91-1.jpg` | 1.91:1 | Open Graph / link previews / most article headers |
| `_2-1.jpg` | 2:1 | Wide banners |
| `_2.35-1.jpg` | 2.35:1 | Cinematic letterbox |

7 screenshots × 3 ratios. Originals are in `../full/` (PNG, 1920×1080).

**Need a ratio that isn't here? Ask and we'll cut it.** Please don't centre-crop the
16:9 yourself — the mechanic drops out of the picture.

## A note on `05_crowd-on-fire`

Included for completeness, but it's a full-screen gold flash. At thumbnail size it can
read as a rendering glitch rather than an effect, so we'd suggest not leading with it.

## Portrait (1:1, 4:5) is not included

Going taller means cropping the sides, which cuts one of the two fighters — and "both
players reveal at the same time" stops being visible at all. Those need letterboxing
rather than cropping. Ask us and we'll make them.
